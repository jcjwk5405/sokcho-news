Editor's Letter

A Place to Learn Keeps a Place Alive

This week's editorial and debate topic both look at education. The editorial argues that as the population shrinks, schools close, and as schools close, families with children leave. It says Sokcho, Goseong, Yangyang, and Inje should run small schools together and share special programs, so no single county has to solve this alone. Our debate topic asks a related question: with the national exam one month away, should Korea move to pass/fail-style grading instead of ranking students against each other?

Nationally, this week's newspaper editorials were dominated by two topics: the confirmation hearings for two cabinet nominees, and a possible troop deployment to the Strait of Hormuz. President Lee Jae-myung made a state visit to France from September 6 to 9, and agreed with President Macron to work together on freedom of navigation through the Strait. On September 9, Korea's stock index closed above 7,000 points for the first time in 33 trading days.

This week's opinion section covers both Gallup Korea and the National Barometer Survey (NBS). President Lee's approval rating fell to 38% (Gallup) and 43% (NBS), the lowest since he took office in both polls. Both surveys also found more people saying the two cabinet nominees are unfit for the job than fit, and more people opposed to the Hormuz deployment than in favor.

County Notices

Elder Care, a New Sea Walkway, and a Poet's Centennial

🏙️ Sokcho

👵 Integrated elder care forum

Sokcho held a welfare forum on September 4 on how to support elderly residents as the city becomes a super-aged society. About 150 officials and care workers attended, discussing how to link administrative, health, welfare, and elder-care services together.

💰 Newlywed housing loan support

Sokcho opened a second round of applications for interest support on housing loans for newlywed couples living in the city.

🌊 Goseong

🌊 Songjiho Sea Sky Road opens

Goseong officially opened its 631-meter sea walkway connecting Oho Beach to Jukdo Island on September 5. The county will run it directly through October 31, with admission at 5,000 won for adults.

🎨 Museum staff hiring

Goseong is hiring temporary staff for the Jinburyeong Art Museum. Details are available on the county website.

🏄 Yangyang

🐕 Dog-friendly surf festival

Yangyang held its third "Surfing with Dogs" festival on September 12 at Dongsan Beach, with about 50 dogs and their owners taking part alongside local residents. Young local organizers planned and ran the event.

⛰️ Inje

📖 Manhae centennial exhibition

Inje is running a special exhibition through September 20 at Manhae Village, marking 100 years since the publication of the poet Han Yong-un's "Your Silence," featuring his handwritten calligraphy and writings.

🏘 Apartment facility support

Inje continues offering funding to improve aging facilities — roads, walkways, and community halls — in 78 apartment complexes with a total of 1,940 households.

This Week's Terms

Words in the News, Explained

Three terms that came up often in this week's national newspaper editorials — explained simply, so you can follow the news even if you're new to Korean politics and policy.

Confirmation HearingPOLITICS

A National Assembly hearing to check whether a president's nominee for a top government post is fit for the job. This week, hearings for two cabinet nominees became a major controversy.

Freedom of NavigationSECURITY

The principle that ships from any country should be able to pass freely through a strait or open sea. President Lee stressed this principle while discussing the Strait of Hormuz with France this week.

Pass/Fail GradingEDUCATION

A grading method that judges students only by whether they reach a fixed score, not by ranking them against each other. This is the core idea in this week's debate over reforming Korea's national exam.
