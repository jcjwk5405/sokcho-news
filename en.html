Editor's Letter

When the House Turns on Itself

This week's editorial imagines a Seorak region where the old and the young can both smile — improving elder care and child care together, since a stable older generation lets young parents work and raise their children well. Our debate topic asks a related question: should families or society bear the main responsibility for elder care?

Nationally, the National Assembly passed a criminal procedure bill that added a last-minute clause on case dismissal, prompting the justice minister to offer his resignation. Assembly Speaker Cho Jung-sik's remark that the president's tenure "should be a matter for the public to decide" drew criticism from every major newspaper. The stock market fell sharply during the week, and a new US ambassador arrived amid friction in the Korea-US relationship.

This week's opinion section uses the NBS national survey instead of Gallup, which is pausing its regular polling for two weeks and will return on August 14. The NBS survey found the president's approval rating at 53%, the lowest since taking office.

County Notices

Summer Festivals in Full Swing

🏙️ Sokcho

🎉 Summer festival

The 2026 Sokcho Summer Festival runs from July 31 to August 2 at Sokcho Beach, with performances across several music genres. A larger music festival, Waterbomb Sokcho 2026, follows on August 22.

🌊 Night swimming

Sokcho Beach will stay open for night swimming until August 12, with hours extended to 9 p.m. Safety measures include added lifeguards and lighted buoys.

🕊️ Goseong

🏛 County chief's visits

County Chief Ham Myeong-jun made a series of visits to the National Assembly to push forward Goseong's peace economic zone designation and tourism infrastructure projects.

📢 Notice

The 55th Infantry Brigade has posted its August mortar firing schedule. Nearby residents can check exact dates on the county website.

🏄 Yangyang

🎉 Festival wrap-up

The 2026 Yangyang Summer Festival, held at Naksan Beach, returned after a seven-year hiatus with performances by Hi-Key, Jadu, and other artists.

🏄 New festival

The 2026 Hajodae Beach Festival opened August 1 at Hajodae, a well-known surfing spot, with activities and performances themed around the beach.

⛰️ Inje

🏁 Ongoing event

Motorsport events continue through August at Inje Speedium, drawing summer tourists.

💧 Local issue

County Chief Choi Sang-gi is again pressing the province and central government, ahead of parliamentary budget review, for a fairer share of Han River Basin Fund money.

This Week's Terms

Words in the News, Explained

Three terms that came up often in this week's national newspaper editorials — explained simply, so you can follow the news even if you're new to Korean politics and policy.

Case DismissalLAW

When a court ends a case without ruling on its substance because of a problem with how the charges were filed. This week's debate over a new criminal procedure clause centered on this kind of dismissal.

Circuit Breaker ("Sidecar")ECONOMY

A safeguard that temporarily halts program-trading orders when stock prices swing sharply, to calm the market. It drew fresh attention as the Kospi index became more volatile this week.

Special InspectorPOLITICS

An independent official who investigates wrongdoing by the president's family members or close aides. This week's editorials argued the next appointee should be someone "most uncomfortable for those in power."
